# Paper B1 — outline (one key sentence per section)

Working title: *Nonreciprocal spin couplings and the Doppler breakdown of driven chiral patterns*
Target: Phys. Rev. B (regular article, REVTeX 4-2). Companion: paper B2 (beyond threshold); foundational paper on nonreciprocal spin couplings.

Core statement (abstract's last sentence): a fixed nonreciprocal bias acts on a chiral pattern only through two vectors of opposite time-reversal parity; this fixes its first-order distortions and replaces Walker breakdown by a Doppler (Landau) instability of the pattern's Goldstone mode, whose threshold is set by the dispersion order and by a static Doppler efficiency λ.
